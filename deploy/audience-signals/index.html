Features

What Audience Signals actually does

Every stage of your event gives off signals, what people say before it starts, how they engage during it, what they need after. Here's how Audience Signals captures all three.

01

Set up your Engagement Profile

Tell Audience Signals what your event is about: your audience, your objectives, your call to action. Everything else you build from here, questions, an assessment, AI suggestions, uses this same context.

02

Define your engagement questions

Decide what you actually want to learn from this audience, their goals, their blockers, what's stopping them from buying. AI can suggest a full set of questions, tuned to your event and your offer.

03

Build a scored self-assessment

Send it out before the event to start building rapport early, or offer it live for anyone who didn't get to it. A short, scored set of questions shows you exactly where each person stands.

04

Automatically analyzes the chat

After the event, Audience Signals reads the entire chat and surfaces the patterns, recurring challenges, shared goals, objections nobody said to your face. Every insight links back to the real messages behind it.

05

Every planned question, answered

See exactly how your audience responded to each question you planted, organized and summarized, instead of scrolling through hundreds of messages looking for the good ones.

06

Know how to follow up with them

Go attendee by attendee and see what Audience Signals picked up about each one, in their own words, so your auto-generated sales follow-up message already knows what matters to them.

Before the event

Know who's in the room before you start teaching

Give participants an easy way to tell you where they are, what they're struggling with, and what matters most to them, before you ever open your event. Send it out ahead of time, in your registration or confirmation emails, or offer it live for anyone who skips it. Either way, you're not starting with assumptions.

01

Know before they arrive

Sent with registration, the assessment starts surfacing who's ready, what's holding them back, and where to focus your attention, before the event even starts.

02

Catch everyone else live

Anyone who didn't complete it ahead of time can still take it during the event, so your data covers the whole room, not just the early responders.

03

See exactly where they stand

A short, scored set of questions shows you who's furthest along, what's blocking each person, and who's most ready for what you're offering next.

Every response feeds directly into Participant Intelligence, so your first follow-up message already knows exactly where each person stands.

During the event

Turn engagement into insight

Most presenters use questions just to keep people involved. Audience Signals goes further: purposeful engagement questions that get participants sharing their challenges, goals, interests, and objections right in the chat. Every answer is telling you something, this is how you capture it.

01

Built from your Engagement Profile

Because Audience Signals already knows your audience, your offer, and your objectives from your Engagement Profile, AI can suggest a full set of questions tuned specifically to this event, not generic prompts.

02

Matched automatically, reviewed when it's close

Attendee responses get matched to the right question automatically. Anything uncertain gets flagged for a quick review, so nothing gets attributed to the wrong person.

03

Organized by question, not buried in chat

Every response lands under the question it answers, summarized and ready to read, instead of scattered across hundreds of messages.

Every answer here becomes part of Engagement Analysis: one clear summary per question, evidence-linked back to who actually said it.

After the event

Follow up with what they actually told you

After the event, Audience Signals brings together everything a participant shared, their assessment answers, their engagement responses, into one picture, then drafts a follow-up built from what they actually said. Less generic follow-up. More relevant conversations.

01

Built from what was actually learned

Each draft pulls from that person's own Participant Intelligence findings and your event's offer, not just their raw chat messages.

02

Choose your channel

Generate an email, a LinkedIn message, or a direct message, each written in your own voice and tone, ready in one click.

03

Edit, regenerate, or refine

Every draft is a starting point, not a send button. Make it shorter, more conversational, or more professional, or regenerate it entirely, in one click.

The result: a sales follow-up that already knows what matters to them, ready before you'd have finished reading the chat yourself.
