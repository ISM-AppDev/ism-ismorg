## The problem

Owners tweak their offers constantly — new bonus, new price, new name — with no way to tell
whether a change made the offer stronger or just different. Superficial "offer score"
tools grade the wording of a sales page. Nobody checks the thing that decides whether the
offer sells: does it match a problem this audience will pay to solve, and is it clearly
better than the alternatives, including doing nothing.

## What it does

The user enters an offer — existing or proposed — and the tool evaluates it in the context
of the stored audience and the owner's other offers:

- Problem-to-offer alignment and audience fit
- Clarity, specificity, and perceived value
- Deliverables and buyer friction
- Credibility and proof
- Pricing context relative to the outcome and to the rest of the portfolio
- Likely objections and how the offer answers them
- Differentiation from substitutes and from inaction

It prioritizes the two or three issues that most affect whether the offer converts, rather
than returning a long even-weighted checklist, and gives concrete recommendations.

## Who it is for

Owners who are packaging a new offer, repricing an existing one, or trying to work out why
a service that "should" sell isn't. Also useful before a launch, as a last gut-check.

## How it works

1. A tool in the **Impact Intelligence** platform. Uses shared auth and the Business
   Intelligence Profile, including its multi-offer records.
2. The user picks an existing offer from the profile or drafts a new one inline.
3. The model analyzes it against audience, audience problems, objections, and sibling
   offers, then returns prioritized issues + fixes and an overall read (strong / mixed / weak).
4. Results are stored as an `offer_analysis` so the Lead Magnet Opportunity Finder and the
   Impact Intelligence Brief can build on them ("your core offer's weak point is proof").

## Why it is worth building

- Offers are where revenue is won or lost; a tool that improves them earns its keep fast.
- It naturally pairs with Message Market-Fit Checker — fix the offer, then fix the message
  that sells it.
- Portfolio-aware analysis (is this offer cannibalizing that one?) is something a generic
  chatbot can't do without the stored context Impact Intelligence already has.
- Sets up a clean path into the What Should I Promote logic.

## Build phases

### Phase 1 — Single-offer diagnostic
Structured offer input (name, audience, problem, deliverable, price, CTA). Prioritized
issues + recommendations against a minimal audience object. Store the analysis.

### Phase 2 — Portfolio- and profile-aware
Pull offers, audience, and objections from the Business Intelligence Profile. Compare the offer against
siblings for overlap and laddering. Progressive profiling for gaps.

### Phase 3 — Cross-tool and longitudinal
Feed weak points to the Lead Magnet Opportunity Finder and the Impact Intelligence Brief. Track an
offer's analysis history so the owner can see whether edits actually helped.

## Stack and dependencies

- Impact Intelligence shell, auth, entitlement gate, Business Intelligence Profile (audience + multi-offer records)
- Anthropic API with a fixed evaluation rubric; structured output
- `offer_analyses` table: offer snapshot, verdicts, prioritized issues, recommendations, links to related runs
- No external research needed for the base tool

## Open questions and risks

- **Rubric stability.** Needs a consistent scoring frame so re-runs are comparable — worth
  an eval pass before launch.
- **Draft vs stored offers.** Editing a stored offer inside the analyzer should optionally
  write back to the profile, but not silently.
- **Pricing advice boundary.** The tool comments on pricing *context*, not "charge $X" —
  keep it out of prescriptive financial advice.
- **Overlap with human judgment.** Position as a structured second opinion, not a verdict.
