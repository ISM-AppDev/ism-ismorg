## The problem

Happy customers rarely write testimonials. A blank box and "tell us about your experience"
is too much work, so you chase people for weeks and get a trickle of thin one-liners. The
genuinely good social proof stays stuck in people's heads.

## What it does

You send your customer one link. They answer a handful of tap-to-answer questions about
their experience: how it went overall, the biggest benefit, how likely they are to
recommend it, and what they'd tell someone considering it. No typing. From those choices it
drafts a polished testimonial in their voice, grounded in what you actually offer rather
than generic praise. They can tweak the wording, then send it. It lands in your queue, and
**nothing goes public until you approve it.**

## Who it is for

Any owner who runs on social proof — service businesses, coaches, speakers, event hosts,
agencies, software — and finds that collecting testimonials is a constant, low-yield chore.

## How it works

1. **Add an offering.** A product, service, event, speaker, or your business as a whole,
   plus its web address so the drafts reflect what it really is.
2. **Share one link.** It never expires and can be reused: in a text, an email, a follow-up
   sequence, or a QR code on your table.
3. **Your customer taps through** three to five quick questions, worded for that kind of
   offering.
4. **AI writes the draft** from their answers and your offering's context. They see exactly
   what they are sending and can edit it first.
5. **You review.** Every submission arrives as New. Edit it, approve it, or archive it.
6. **Show the approved ones on your website** with a snippet that works in most site
   builders and stays up to date as you add or remove testimonials.

It runs on testimonial credits that don't expire, with no subscription needed. An optional
paid upgrade, Embedded Testimonial Pro, adds more control over how the website display
looks. Without it, everything above still works.

## What turned out to matter

- **The blank page was the whole problem.** Tap-through instead of write-from-scratch is what
  makes customers actually finish.
- **Approval is built in, twice.** The customer approves their own wording, and the owner
  approves before anything is public, which also keeps it clear of fabricated-testimonial
  problems.
- **Grounding the draft in a real page** about the offering is what keeps the drafts from all
  sounding alike.
