## The problem

You meet people worth knowing in an online event. Someone shares a LinkedIn profile, says
they want to connect, or mentions a problem you could help with. You save the chat. Then you
get busy.

A few days later you have to work out who they were, what they said, why you wanted to
contact them, and how to reach them. The chat becomes another file you saved and never
really used. The information is not missing. It is buried.

## What it does

Chat Navigator keeps each person together with their conversation, and answers the four
questions you actually have after the meeting:

- **Who** should I follow up with?
- **Why** did I want to contact them?
- **How** do I reach them?
- **What** do I say?

It never sends anything for you. You edit the draft, copy it, and send it yourself.

## Who it is for

Anyone who comes out of webinars, summits, masterminds, and networking calls with a saved
chat full of people they meant to get back to, and no good way to find them again.

## How it works

1. **Bring in the chat.** A saved Zoom or Teams chat, a CSV, or a pasted list. No
   integrations to set up.
2. **Search by what people said.** Ask in plain words, for example "people who mentioned
   needing help with hiring", and each match shows exactly what that person wrote.
3. **See the card.** A follow-up card holds the person, the context of what they said, and
   the email addresses, LinkedIn profiles, and links they shared in the chat.
4. **Get a first draft.** A message based on their own words, with quick options to make it
   shorter, more conversational, or more professional.
5. **Mark it done** when you have followed up, so the list shows who is still waiting.

## What is live today

- Zoom and Teams chat imports, CSVs and pasted lists, and attendee-directory PDFs
- Search across a chat by what people said, with the exact messages shown
- Follow-up cards that pull out shared emails and LinkedIn links, with notes and priority
- First-draft messages with tone options, always sent by you and never by the tool
- A free trial, and a Pro tier that matches people across events and uses that history in
  the drafts
- Automatic deletion of events after 12 months, with reminders and an export first

## What turned out to matter

- **Not every name is a lead.** The value is in helping decide who deserves another
  conversation while the context is still available, not in a longer list.
- **Keeping the person and what they said together** is what makes a follow-up feel
  personal instead of generic.
- **Nothing sends on its own.** It drafts; the owner decides.
