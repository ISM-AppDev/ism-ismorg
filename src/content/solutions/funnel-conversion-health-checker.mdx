## The problem

Traffic arrives, few people convert, and the owner can't tell which page or step is the
problem. They rewrite headlines on instinct and hope. There is no structured audit of the
funnel — the sales page, the opt-in, the nurture emails, the booking or checkout step — so
the same leaks stay unfixed for months.

## What it does

Assesses the main components of the funnel for friction, leaks, and underperformance, and
flags concrete issues:

- Slow lead response times
- Unclear, buried, or competing calls to action
- A mismatch between the ad or link promise and what the page delivers
- Common abandonment spots and dead ends
- Missing or weak follow-up after an opt-in
- Mobile and load-speed problems

For each it gives a plain fix and a best-practice benchmark, and it ranks them so the owner
knows what to change first.

## Who it is for

Owners running a self-serve or semi-serve funnel — a course, a productized service, a
booking flow — without a conversion specialist on the team.

## How it works

1. A tool inside the Impact Intelligence platform. The owner points it at their funnel:
   page URLs plus a short description of the sequence and where traffic comes from.
2. It reads each page and checks it against a fixed conversion rubric and benchmark set,
   treating page content as untrusted input, never as instructions.
3. It cross-references the Business Intelligence Profile — who the page is for, what the
   offer is, the core promise — to catch audience and message mismatch a generic checker
   would miss.
4. It returns per-asset findings, a ranked "fix this first" list, and rewrite suggestions
   for the worst CTAs and sections.
5. A flagged page can be handed straight to the Message Market-Fit Checker for a deeper
   copy diagnostic.

## Why it is worth building

- Conversion is where spent traffic is won or lost; a structured audit pays for itself in
  one fixed leak.
- It pairs naturally with Lead Pipeline Diagnostics (that finds the volume gap, this finds
  the conversion gap) and feeds the Action Advisor.
- Profile-aware auditing — checking the page against the *stated* audience and promise — is
  the differentiator over generic "CRO checklist" tools.
- Tiering: base = one-time audit with fixes; upgrade = benchmarks, re-audits to track
  improvement, and regression alerts.

## Build phases

### Phase 1 — Page + sequence audit
Owner supplies URLs and describes the flow. Audit each asset against a fixed rubric; return
prioritized fixes and CTA/section rewrites. Cross-check against the profile for
message-audience fit.

### Phase 2 — Benchmarks and inputs
Owner adds response-time and abandonment data (or a light analytics/tag connection).
Industry benchmark comparisons. Re-audit on demand to measure whether a fix worked.

### Phase 3 — Continuous watch
Re-check pages on a schedule; alert on regressions (page changed, load time up, CTA
removed). Feed "we just changed X" into Scheduled Posts as a content prompt.

## Stack and dependencies

- Impact Intelligence shell, auth, entitlement gate, Business Intelligence Profile
- Page fetch + parse with prompt-injection isolation of fetched content
- A conversion rubric and an industry benchmark set (build and maintain)
- Anthropic API for findings and rewrites; structured output per asset
- `funnel_audits`, `findings` tables

## Open questions and risks

- **Reading a page is not knowing its analytics.** True conversion and bounce data needs an
  integration or owner input; Phase 1 findings are heuristic and should say so.
- **Benchmark credibility.** Numbers need a defensible source or they undermine trust.
- **JS-heavy / gated pages.** Some funnels can't be fetched cleanly; need a graceful
  "describe it instead" path.
- **Scope creep** toward being a full analytics product — stay a diagnostic that points at
  fixes.
