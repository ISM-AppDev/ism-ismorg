## The problem

Reacting to news is one of the most reliable ways to get reach, and it is almost impossible
to do on a schedule. By the time an owner notices a relevant story, reads it, works out an
angle that fits their audience, and writes something, the moment has passed — or they just
never see the story because they are not sitting in a feed all day. Trend-research sites
help with discovery but stop there: no filter for *this* business, no drafts, no follow-through.

## What it does

A daily pass over the news, narrowed to the owner's world and turned into usable drafts:

- The owner sets their **industry**, the **news categories** to watch, and the **topics
  and keywords their audience cares about** (plus sources to trust or mute).
- Each morning the system scans current stories in those categories, clusters duplicates,
  and **ranks them by relevance to the stored audience** and by how much angle potential
  they have.
- For the top handful it produces: a plain-English "why this matters to your audience," two
  or three angles, and **multiple drafted posts** — a LinkedIn post, a LinkedIn article
  outline, a short-form take — with suggested hashtags.
- The owner skims, picks the ones worth using, edits, and sends them on — later, straight
  into the Scheduled Posts queue or out to LinkedIn.

The bar is: open it with coffee, leave five minutes later with two posts worth publishing.

## Who it is for

Consultants, founders, and executives building authority in a specific lane who know
newsjacking works but can't sustain the daily discovery-to-draft loop by hand. Strong fit
alongside Scheduled Posts, which gives the drafts somewhere to land.

## How it works

1. A tool in the **Impact Intelligence** platform, using shared auth and the Business
   Intelligence Profile. Setup captures industry, watched categories, audience keywords,
   tone, sources, and how many drafts per day the owner wants.
2. A scheduled morning job ingests stories from news feeds and APIs for the watched
   categories, deduplicates and clusters near-identical coverage, and attaches source
   metadata and timestamps.
3. A relevance pass scores each cluster against the stored audience, keywords, and the
   owner's positioning; low scorers are dropped.
4. For the survivors, the model generates the "why it matters," angles, and drafts —
   treating the article text as untrusted input, never as instructions.
5. The owner reviews the daily set: use, edit, "more like this," or dismiss. Chosen drafts
   go to Scheduled Posts or straight out (Phase 3). Decisions train the relevance filter.

Modeled on what a tool like *NewsJacking Daily* surfaces — trending topics with hooks,
ready-to-post drafts, and per-platform prompts — but filtered to one business and wired
through to review and publishing rather than left as a public research library.

## Why it is worth building

- It is a high-reach content source that no competitor tool personalizes. Generic trend
  feeds give everyone the same angles; this one is scored against the owner's audience.
- It compounds with the rest of the Brand & Visibility set: Newsjack produces drafts,
  Scheduled Posts queues and publishes them, Message Market-Fit Checker can pre-flight the
  risky ones.
- The relevance filter improving from the owner's use/dismiss history is a moat and a
  retention hook — it gets visibly better, and leaving resets it.
- Clear tiering: base = a daily list you copy from; upgrade = auto-drafting into the queue,
  breaking-story alerts, and scheduled publishing.

## Build phases

### Phase 1 — Daily relevant list
Category + keyword setup. Once-daily job: pull stories, cluster, score against the audience,
show the top 5 with "why it matters" and **one** drafted LinkedIn post each. Manual copy-out.
Store stories, drafts, and use/dismiss decisions.

### Phase 2 — Formats, tuning, and hand-off
Multiple formats per story (post, article outline, short-form). Tone control. Source
allow/mute list. "More like this / less like this" feeding the relevance model. Push a
chosen draft into the Scheduled Posts queue.

### Phase 3 — Alerts and publishing
Off-cycle "breaking" alerts for stories that clear a high relevance bar before the next
daily run. Scheduled/direct publishing to LinkedIn. Performance feedback → learn which
topics and angles land. Brand-safety filter that suppresses tragedy/disaster stories and
flags sensitive ones for manual review.

## Stack and dependencies

- Impact Intelligence shell, auth, entitlement gate, Business Intelligence Profile (audience, keywords, positioning, tone)
- News ingestion: RSS + a news API (licensing and coverage matter — see risks); dedup / clustering
- Relevance scoring against stored audience + keywords; stored feedback loop
- Anthropic API for "why it matters," angles, and drafts, with strict prompt-injection isolation of article text; fact-vs-take separation
- `news_items`, `news_clusters`, `newsjack_drafts` tables; feedback / decisions store
- Scheduler / job runner (shared); LinkedIn API + OAuth (Phase 3); integration with Scheduled Posts

## Open questions and risks

- **News data.** Free RSS is patchy; a proper news API has real cost and licensing terms
  (and limits on storing/reproducing text). This is the biggest unknown for the build.
- **Relevance precision.** Two junk stories in the daily five and the owner stops opening
  it. The scoring pass has to be conservative — better to show 3 great than 5 mediocre.
- **Brand safety.** Newsjacking a tragedy is a reputational disaster. Phase 3 needs a hard
  suppression list plus a "sensitive — review manually" flag well before auto-publish.
- **Timeliness.** A once-daily pass is too slow for fast-moving stories; that is what the
  Phase 3 breaking alerts are for, but they raise the noise risk.
- **Prompt injection.** Article pages are hostile input — the pipeline must quarantine
  fetched text from the instruction context.
- **Sameness.** If every user in an industry gets similar source stories, the angles have
  to be what differentiates — lean hard on the owner's positioning, not just the headline.
