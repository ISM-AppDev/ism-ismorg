## The problem

Owners with more than one offer waste weeks half-promoting all of them. Every offer feels
urgent; none gets a real push. The question isn't "write me a campaign" — it's "given my
portfolio, the season, what's coming up, and what's happening in the market, *which one
deserves the spotlight this month, and why now?*"

## What it does

The tool weighs the owner's offer portfolio, current priorities, recent promotions,
seasonality, upcoming events, Audience Pulse findings, and business goals, then returns a
**decision**:

- A recommended primary promotion, with an explanation of why now is the right time
- A messaging angle
- The most relevant audience segment
- An appropriate call to action
- Optionally, a secondary promotion when one clearly earns it

It is decision support, not a content generator. The output fits on a card and the owner
can act on it or overrule it.

## Who it is for

Owners on the higher Impact Intelligence tiers with three-plus live offers who keep second-guessing their focus.
Best used monthly, or whenever a launch window opens.

## How it works

1. A tool in **Impact Intelligence**, on the higher plan tiers.
2. Reads the offer portfolio, priorities, recent promotion history, and calendar from the
   shared profile.
3. Pulls the latest Audience Pulse findings for current market context.
4. The model scores each candidate promotion on timing, audience readiness, portfolio fit,
   and goal alignment, then recommends one (or two) with reasoning.
5. The recommendation is stored as a `promotion_recommendation`; the Impact Intelligence Brief can
   carry it forward and check whether the owner acted.

## Why it is worth building

- It's the connective tissue of the higher tiers — it consumes Audience Pulse and the
  portfolio and produces a single next action.
- It answers a question owners actually ask out loud, in plain language, which makes the
  value obvious and easy to sell.
- Low build cost relative to Audience Pulse and the Brief, because it orchestrates existing
  context rather than doing fresh research.

## Build phases

### Phase 1 — Portfolio + calendar
Score offers on priority, recency of last promotion, and upcoming events. Recommend one
primary with reasoning, angle, segment, CTA. Store it.

### Phase 2 — Market-aware
Ingest Audience Pulse findings as a timing input. Add "why now" grounded in current signals.
Optional secondary recommendation.

### Phase 3 — Closed loop
Feed the recommendation into the Impact Intelligence Brief. Track acted / ignored. Learn the
owner's overrides over time and reflect them back ("you've skipped the course three months
running — deprioritize it?").

## Stack and dependencies

- Impact Intelligence shell, auth, **higher-tier entitlement gate**, Business Intelligence Profile (offers, priorities, promotion history, events)
- Anthropic API; structured output for the recommendation card
- `promotion_recommendations` table: candidate set, scores, chosen promotion, reasoning, acted flag, timestamp
- Depends on Audience Pulse for the market-timing input (Phase 2+)

## Open questions and risks

- **Garbage in.** Needs the owner to keep priorities and the event calendar current, or the
  recommendation is guesswork. Progressive prompts help.
- **Confidence framing.** Must read as "here's the case for X," not a guarantee.
- **Thin without Pulse.** Phase 1 alone may feel obvious. The value shows up once market
  timing is in the mix.
- **Override handling.** Learning from overrides is powerful but easy to get creepy — keep
  it transparent.
