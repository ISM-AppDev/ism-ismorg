## The problem

"Make a lead magnet" advice produces generic checklists that attract the wrong people and
connect to nothing the business sells. Owners end up with a PDF that gets downloads and no
pipeline. The missing step is strategic: pick a problem the *right* prospect has, solve a
slice of it for free, and make the paid offer the obvious next move.

## What it does

Using the stored audience, audience problems, existing offers, existing lead magnets, and
current business priorities, the tool proposes **specific** lead-magnet opportunities — not
content themes. For each one it gives:

- The problem it addresses and why that problem matters now
- Recommended format (assessment, calculator, template, teardown, mini-course, checklist…)
- A proposed title
- The core content / what's actually inside
- The intended outcome for the prospect
- The call to action
- The natural next paid offer it ladders into

It preserves the path **Problem → Lead Magnet → Next Step → Offer** for every suggestion,
and it avoids duplicating lead magnets the owner already has.

## Who it is for

Owners running lean funnels who know they need better top-of-funnel bait but don't have a
strategist to design it. Especially useful right after the Offer Strength Analyzer, when
the paid offer is solid and now needs feeders.

## How it works

1. A tool in the **Impact Intelligence** platform, using shared auth and the Business Intelligence Profile.
2. Reads audience + problems + offers + existing marketing assets + current priorities from
   the Business Intelligence Profile.
3. Generates a ranked set of lead-magnet opportunities, each fully specified and tied to an
   offer. The owner marks ones as "building" or dismisses them.
4. Chosen opportunities are stored as `lead_magnet_recommendations`; the Impact Intelligence Brief
   can later nudge ("you flagged the pricing calculator three weeks ago — still worth it?").

## Why it is worth building

- Directly addresses the complaint owners voice most: "I need more leads."
- Reuses context no generic tool has — the actual offer ladder and the audience's real
  problems — so the output is usable, not inspirational.
- Completes the acquisition trio with Offer Strength Analyzer and Message Market-Fit
  Checker: strong offer, feeder that leads to it, message that sells it.

## Build phases

### Phase 1 — Opportunity generator
Input: audience, top problems, one target offer. Output: 3–5 fully specified lead-magnet
concepts with the Problem→Magnet→Next→Offer chain. Store selections.

### Phase 2 — Portfolio-aware and de-duplicated
Pull the full offer ladder and existing lead magnets from the profile. Rank by fit to
current priorities. Never re-suggest an asset the owner already has.

### Phase 3 — Cross-tool
Take offer weak points from the Offer Strength Analyzer as inputs. Feed chosen concepts to
the Impact Intelligence Brief for follow-up. Optional: hand a concept to a drafting step that
produces the first version of the asset.

## Stack and dependencies

- Impact Intelligence shell, auth, entitlement gate, Business Intelligence Profile (audience, problems, offers, marketing assets, priorities)
- Anthropic API; structured output so each opportunity renders as a card with fixed fields
- `lead_magnet_recommendations` table: concept fields, linked offer, status (suggested / building / dismissed), timestamps
- No external research for the base tool

## Open questions and risks

- **Format bias.** The model will over-suggest checklists and PDFs; the prompt needs to
  push toward interactive formats (assessments, calculators) where they fit.
- **Too many ideas.** Five good ones beat fifteen. Cap the output.
- **Build gap.** Suggesting a lead magnet is easy; the owner still has to make it. Phase 3
  drafting assist matters for perceived value.
- **Priority drift.** Recommendations should re-rank when the profile's "current priority"
  offer changes.
