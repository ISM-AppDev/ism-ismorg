## The problem

Every tool hands the owner more to do. What they lack is the answer to one question: *of
everything I could work on, what actually moves the needle this week?* Insight without
prioritization is just a longer to-do list, and a longer to-do list is why owners freeze.

## What it does

Synthesizes the latest findings and open recommendations from across the Impact
Intelligence tools — pipeline gaps, funnel leaks, pricing, retention risks, message fit,
audience shifts — and each week:

- Names the **single highest-leverage area** to focus on
- Gives the **one concrete action to take today**
- Explains **why this, why now**
- Links straight into the tool that does the work

A deliberately small dashboard: one priority, one action, the reasoning, and a way to act
on it — so the owner always knows the next most important move even when the week is noisy.

## Who it is for

Owners running Impact Intelligence with several tools producing findings and no time to sit
down and reconcile them into a plan.

## How it works

1. A capability inside the Impact Intelligence platform that reads the current outputs and
   open recommendations from every other tool, plus the Business Intelligence Profile and
   the owner's stated goal.
2. It scores candidate actions by expected impact, effort, and urgency against that goal.
3. It picks one, and presents it as: *Focus on X. Why: … Do this today: … → open [tool].*
4. It runs weekly, remembers what it recommended and whether it was done, and adjusts —
   surfacing important-but-ignored items again rather than repeating itself.

## Why it is worth building

- It is the payoff layer for the whole platform: the individual tools produce insight, this
  turns the pile of insight into a single decision.
- Weekly "here's your one move" is a strong retention mechanic — it's a reason to log in
  every week, and its usefulness grows as more tools feed it.
- It closes the loop: tracking whether the recommended action got done and what happened is
  the data that makes every other tool smarter over time.
- Tiering: base = aggregated list + this-week pick; upgrade = scoring against the goal,
  carry-over logic, weekly email, and outcome tracking.

## Build phases

### Phase 1 — Aggregated focus
Collect open recommendations from the other tools into one ranked list. Surface a single
"this week" pick with a next action and a link into the relevant tool. Manual "done / skip."

### Phase 2 — Scored and delivered
Impact / effort / urgency scoring against the stated goal. "Why now" reasoning. Carry-over
logic so ignored-but-important items resurface. A weekly email digest.

### Phase 3 — Closed loop
Track the outcomes of completed actions. Learn the owner's follow-through patterns and
preferences and tune the ranking. Optional Slack or email delivery.

## Stack and dependencies

- Impact Intelligence shell, auth, entitlement gate, Business Intelligence Profile
- Read access to every other tool's recommendation store (a shared contract for "open
  recommendations")
- A scoring model (impact × urgency ÷ effort, weighted by goal alignment)
- Anthropic API for the synthesis and the "why now" rationale
- `focus_recommendations`, `action_log` tables
- Scheduler / job runner (shared); email delivery (Resend) for Phase 2

## Open questions and risks

- **Only as good as its inputs.** With one tool in use it has nothing to synthesize —
  needs a sensible fallback and a nudge to adopt more tools.
- **"One thing" can feel reductive** if the reasoning isn't visible. The *why* has to be as
  prominent as the *what*.
- **Repetition.** Recommending the same area every week (because it's genuinely the
  biggest) will feel broken — needs progress tracking and a way to say "still #1, here's the
  next step within it."
- **Overlap with What Should I Promote.** That tool is promotion-specific; this one is
  whole-business. Keep the boundary explicit or they'll seem redundant.
